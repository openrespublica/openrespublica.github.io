Who we are

OpenResPublica is a civic technology initiative that makes public documents verifiable and auditable. We combine cryptographic hashing, immutable logging, and a citizen‑facing portal so anyone can confirm the integrity of official records.

Mission

To empower citizens, auditors, and public servants with accessible, tamper‑evident records that strengthen accountability.

Vision

A Philippines where every public document is verifiable, every transaction leaves an immutable trail, and trust is measurable.

Approach

We stamp documents with cryptographic fingerprints, log them in an immutable store, and publish metadata to a public ledger for easy verification.

Who benefits

Citizens, journalists, auditors, civil society, and government agencies seeking transparent, auditable records.

Core values

  • Integrity — Immutable evidence of authenticity.
  • Accessibility — Simple interfaces for everyone.
  • Transparency — Public ledger and open processes.
  • Collaboration — Working with government and civic tech.
