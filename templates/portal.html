01 — System Status

The ORP Engine is operational. Documents submitted through the PDF Stamp or PhilID Ingest routes are automatically hashed, anchored to the local immudb instance, and published to the public ledger at openrespublica.github.io within 60 to 90 seconds of issuance.

🔒 This portal is restricted to authorized barangay operators only. All access and issuance actions are logged and anchored to the immutable ledger.
🔗 Public ledger syncs to openrespublica.github.io within 60–90 seconds. Records are cryptographically immutable once pushed.
