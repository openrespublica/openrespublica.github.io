About the Project

The OpenResPublica initiative for Barangay Bunao leverages immudb technology to provide a tamper-proof record of all issued clearances and certifications. This system protects residents against identity theft and document forgery.

Mission

To digitize barangay services while maintaining the highest standards of data privacy and cryptographic integrity.

Vision

A future where every resident can prove their residency and status instantly, securely, and without bureaucratic delay.
